Boots, menus, the opening cinematic and gameplay (the Erlang tutorial fight) all render correctly. Walking, camera, lock-on and attack prompts work. No crashes or GPU faults on a warm shader cache.

- **Very slow:** about 2 fps in the cinematic and in gameplay, with multi-second stalls. Pipelines are compiled again on every boot (481 during the cinematic, 182 in 4 minutes of gameplay); a fix is in progress on sharpemu-mac.
- **First boot with an empty shader cache:** Unreal's watchdog stops the game on the loading screen (`GameThread timed out waiting for RenderThread after 120.00 secs`) while shaders compile. Later boots get through.
- Settings: `SHARPEMU_DISPLAY_RESOLUTION=1080p`, `SHARPEMU_HLE_FAST_PATH=1`.
